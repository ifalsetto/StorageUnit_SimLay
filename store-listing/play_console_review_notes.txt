StorageUnit SimLay is an inventory and resale evidence workflow app for storage-unit buyers and resellers.

Test flow for reviewers:
1. Open the app.
2. Create a new inventory run.
3. Upload or stage a sample storage-unit/product image.
4. Review detected or manually entered item.
5. Add price evidence as URL/screenshot/manual structured evidence.
6. Export Wix CSV and audit JSON.

The app needs camera/media permissions only for user-selected storage-unit photos and videos. It does not run background capture.

If AI vision is disabled in the review environment, use the mock provider/sample flow. Live market connectors are disabled unless explicitly configured with approved credentials.
