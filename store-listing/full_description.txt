StorageUnit SimLay is an accuracy-first inventory companion for storage-unit buyers, resellers, and small operators.

Capture photos or walkthrough media, review detected items, add structured price evidence, and export a Wix-compatible product CSV with an audit trail that explains what was known, what was uncertain, and why each item was priced or left blank.

Core features:
• Photo and video intake workflow
• Item review with certainty labels
• Manual evidence locker for sold and active comps
• Screenshot evidence support
• Conservative valuation gates
• Wix CSV export
• Audit JSON export
• Config-driven profiles for different projects
• No silent guessing: unclear items stay uncertain

StorageUnit SimLay is built for defensible resale workflows. The app favors accuracy and auditability over inflated estimates.
